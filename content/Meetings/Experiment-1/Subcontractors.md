The basic concept here is that we have different characters who can do different things, these characters are "contractors". As the users progress through the levels, they unlock new subcontractors and also can improve their affinity with subcontractors. 

# Set up
We will have a list of 10 subcontractors that are viewable from the start; only 3 will be available at level 1.
## Unlock flow
1. Level 1 - 3 available
2. Level 2 - 4 available
3. Level 3 - 6 available
4. Level 4 - 8 available
5. Level 5 - 10 available

## List
1. `SpaceX` - provides launch facilities, spacecraft later (heavy spacecraft)
2. `Rocketlab` - provides the spaceships
3. `Astroforge` - provides mining & smelting capacities
4. `Swinburne Automotive` - provides rover components
5. `Karman+` - builds hardware for mining
6. `Skybus transport`
More to come... 

# Tasks subcontractors [can] perform
1. Buying resources (minerals) - either in space or elsewhere. Reward is lower the earlier the sale is made (so from mining to orbiting Earth/another station to landing on Earth....there's two sale points (when the rocket is orbiting Earth and when it's landed on Earth). The first sale point provides 20% lower reward than the next one)
	1. Smelting
	2. Salvaging
2. Selling resources to the user - at a +15% increased price compared to the usual sale price
	1. Not just raw materials, but also things like food, oxygen, etc
3. Selling ships to the user
	1. This is where the starter ships come from
4. Selling ship components to the user
	1. When users unlock the factories
(So in the early game, pretty much most of what the user does will come from subcontractors rather than from raw materials that have been processed/collected by the user).
5. Construction of factories & other structures
	1. Later, users will be able to hire their own staff to do this
6. Training of asteroids, supplying asteroids
