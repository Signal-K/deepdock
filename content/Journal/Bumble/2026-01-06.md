What's my main problem with Stardew Valley? Well, from a narrative perspective...I don't like backwards "hick towns" or the people in them. Also not really a fan of the anti-corporate message i.e. "corporate jobs are all evil and soul-sucking". Not all of the characters are *that* likeable. 
But from a gameplay perspective...
1. Not enough spaceships
2. Not much industry
3. It gets repetitive a bit - and can be very grindy. It can be a little hard to figure out what to do.....and a lot of the skills or resources are very frustrating to acquire

The idea from [[2026-01-05]] was that you could combine MSM mechanics with a cozy game like SV.
Having singing characters would be a bit of a rip-off...adding a musical element [noteblocks?] is one route. I'm not really sure how this would work...but I'm sure Fred could figure out a solution (or rather, how close we could go to plagiarism....👀).
The mechanics of something like MSM that are good -
1. Collectibles, breeding...(merging)
2. Playful characters with enough differences to 'justify' collecting everything?
3. Immense decoration capacity
