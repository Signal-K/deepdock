## Achievements, Milestones, and Skill Progress

Achievements are persistent badges for players — e.g. "10 Planets Discovered", "5 AI4M Routes Completed", etc. These may unlock new types of missions, tech, or tools.

Milestones are time-sensitive community goals (weekly, per-project), and should award Stardust.

The `/next/{classificationId}` route should eventually show:
- Progress toward related skill badges
- Remaining steps in inspection/follow-up missions
- Additional actions (e.g., bonus missions)

Voting and consensus actions should also contribute to achievements and Stardust rewards.
