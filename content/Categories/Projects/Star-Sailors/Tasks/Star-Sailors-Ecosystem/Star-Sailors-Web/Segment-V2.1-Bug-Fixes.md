## Initial email
Hi!

I'm Liam, I'm the developer and co-founder behind Star Sailors, a citizen science game that you signed up for. I'm reaching out because we're looking for feedback on Star Sailors from our early users as we begin a new chapter in our development.

If you don't remember, Star Sailors is a web game where you complete classification tasks to earn experience and items; helping researchers understand the data they collect. We have all sorts of projects integrated into a single game environment, from discovering exoplanets to tracking the migration paths of endangered & native bird species. You may have been signed up by myself or one of my co-founders, if you're receiving this email.

Star Sailors has always been a community-led hobby project with big goals. At the moment, we're entirely self-funded and this means that we don't have a huge development team - or any development team. Mostly, it's just me. Unfortunately, this means that the UI, mechanics & narrative are designed by an academic, and we would love your feedback on our platform and on citizen science in general. I've attached a survey to this email and we'd love your feedback.

We understand that reaching out to you, possibly two or three years after you tried this weird experiment is awkward. To make up for the cold approach, we're offering three people who complete the survey a CHF 200 VISA gift card. 

If it's been a while since you last logged in, you can play the game here: https://starsailors.space

Finally, thank you so much for signing up and making a contribution to Star Sailors in the past. I'm eternally grateful for your belief in our product and our team.

Regards,

Liam Arbuckle
Developer & Co-Founder, Star Sailors
