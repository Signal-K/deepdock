## Sidebar Scope and Inventory Features

The sidebar serves as the central player inventory and mission tracker. It should display:

- Total Stardust owned
- User's past classifications (shareable)
- Anomalies or missions currently in progress (e.g. satellite inspections)
- Items awaiting community voting or consensus
- Upcoming upgrades (stubbed as “Coming Soon” for now)

Clicking an active mission will open a modal or route with further inspection options or publication actions.
