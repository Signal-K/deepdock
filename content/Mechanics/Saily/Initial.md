I. Narrative Delivery & Chapter Structure
   1. The Briefing: How much narrative text (word count) is the "sweet spot" before a player just wants to get to the puzzle?
A few lines

   2. Chapter Progression: Does each of the 5 chapters increase in puzzle difficulty, or is the difficulty static while the story moves forward?
Static

   3. Character Diversity: Since characters have different origins, do they use different "slang" or UI themes to reflect their world (e.g., a "silicon-consumer" vs. a planetary explorer)?
No...why???

   4. The "Hook": What is the "Cliffhanger" strategy at the end of Chapter 1 to ensure a player returns for Chapter 2 tomorrow?
No idea...there doesn't need to be a cliffhanger. Just the promise of streaks and different puzzles should be enough

   5. Multi-Story Rotation: If there are 4 storylines, are they active simultaneously, or does the whole community play the same story/chapter each day?
All users play through the same storyline, but the chapter is based on where the user is in that story

   6. Narrative Rewards: Is there a "Mission Complete" lore-drop or a visual "souvenir" (like a postcard or data-chip) awarded after finishing a 5-chapter arc?
Yes, it should vary. These can be used to send personalised invites (referral codes - postcards), or repair streaks (datachips), get hints (data-chips), etc.

   7. Story Replayability: If a player misses a day and breaks a streak, can they "catch up" on the story text they missed without playing the puzzle?
The story will return eventually. Streaks can be repaired using data chips

   8. The Player's Role: Since the player is in the "Real World," does the character in the story acknowledge the player as a "remote helper" or just "someone on the other end of the line"?
It's just a person...it doesn't need to be this complicated

   9. Branching Text: Does a "Perfect" score trigger different dialogue than a "barely passed" score?
No. Because we don't know if the user got a perfect score - that's the point...it's citizen science. If we knew what the correct answer was, it wouldn't need to be classified

   10. Atmospheric Sound: In a simple story format, is there a plan for ambient soundscapes (e.g., "starship hum" or "windy plains") to set the mood for the briefing?
Yes, we can have ambience.


  II. Puzzle-Story Synergy
   12. The Goal Mapping: If a character needs "silicon deposits," how is the Mars classification puzzle visually modified to make it feel like "finding silicon" instead of just "tagging craters"?
Well it would be finding deposit types - craters would have different compositions and deposits, wouldn't they?????

   13. Puzzle Type Variety: Can a single 5-chapter story mix puzzle types (e.g., 2 days of Planet Hunter, 3 days of Mars), or is a story locked to one game engine?
We want to utilise multiple puzzle types/projects.

   14. Custom Labels: Do the puzzle labels change based on the story? (e.g., instead of "Transit," the button says "Energy Signature"?)
Well no, because Energy Signature makes less than no sense given the context.

   15. The "Help" Mechanic: If a player uses a "Hint," does the character in the story provide a narrative hint (e.g., "I'm seeing a dip in sector 4")?
Well, no, because again, if we knew the answer, we wouldn't need the user to play and classify the source assets. It should provide an interactive tutorial.

   16. Failure State: If a player fails the puzzle, does the character react with disappointment in the story text?
Yes.

   17. Scientific Accuracy vs. Story: How do you handle cases where a real-world lightcurve has no anomaly, but the character needs to find something for the story to progress?
Move to the next one.

   18. Discovery Feedback: When a player identifies a "Confirmed" planet, how does that event ripple back into the character's narrative (e.g., "You found my new home")?
Yeah pretty much. But remember most of the stories shouldn't just be about finding new homes...that's way too repetitive.

   19. The i-button: What level of "Real-World" scientific detail is shown behind the i-button (e.g., NASA metadata) without breaking the game's immersion?
Just the metadata and image/data source

   20. Story-Specific Tools: Do certain stories unlock "special tools" (like a different zoom level or filter) for that week only?
No

   21. Narrative Urgency: Does the timer (if any) or the scoring system reflect the character’s urgency (e.g., "I'm running out of oxygen, find that deposit fast!")?
No
