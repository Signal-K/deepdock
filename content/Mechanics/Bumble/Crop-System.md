# Crop System

Consolidated from daily notes. References to Stardew Valley and MSM used as design comparison.

## Crop Types

- **Wheat** — first asset set, sprites sourced from AI generation (daily: 2025-12-30)
- Seasonal crops planned — full crop list still pending as of Jan
- Each crop/flower type maps to a specific **honey type** (gameplay loop: grow flowers → bees produce honey → fulfill orders)

## Soil Plots

- Fixed number of soil plots per play area initially
- Expansion via "Allow users to expand plots & hives" story
- Plots should visually look distinct from hives (current SVGs flagged to replace with drawn assets)

## Plant Food

- Mechanic: **pulverize crops** to produce plant food
- Plant food accelerates growth or restores soil
- Source: 2025-11-15 archive note

## Growth Timing

## Design Critique vs Stardew Valley (from 2026-01-06 cafe session with Fred)

Stardew Valley issues noted:
- Too grindy — repetitive loops without narrative payoff
- No spaceships / sci-fi depth
- Insufficient industry/factory depth

**Bumble's differentiation:** shorter play sessions, citizen science integration, bee/pollination as the core hook rather than generic farming

## MSM Mechanic Inspirations (from 2026-01-06)

Elements to borrow from My Singing Monsters:
- **Collectibles** — unlock new bee/crop varieties
- **Breeding** — combine species for new variants
- **Decoration** — cosmetic personalisation of hive/garden area
