The dating app differs from traditional dating apps as traditional dating apps use physical such as face, height and weight in each persons profile. This dating app doesn't show the users face or any physical traits. The app collates the data of the user such as their music playlist, favourite movie and favourite video game, and favourite colours, and people get matched on this app through the AI which matches people to their closest supposed match which it determines through the data it has of each user. This way people can genuinely connect with people's personalities and their interests. The only statistic that this app would rely upon would be the users age, gender, sexuality and the users location. People on the app can filter the preferred age and the preferred gender.  The app is targeted towards people who want a genuine connection and are disillusioned with the idea of modern dating apps. A huge struggle would be to get women into this app. My reasoning to get them in would be that many women get matches on dating apps but the chance of that match turning into a relationship or a connection is extremely low, this dating app matches you to someone not on the basis of looks but on the basis of personality and interests, which is much more likely to get you a genuine connection (this should be able to appeal to a decent amount of women)


App name would be Match. Cause it's pretty catchy. The tagline im still working on.
