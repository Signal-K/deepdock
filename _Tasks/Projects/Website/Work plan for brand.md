## 0. First 10 Minutes (ALL TOGETHER)

**Rule-setting before work starts**

- Agree this session is about **decisions**, not perfection
- One person speaks at a time
- No phones unless required for the task
- Write everything down (Notes / Doc / Whiteboard

Once this is set → start the clock.

---
## FOUNDER A — CONCEPT & STANDARDS (≈ 3 hours)

**Role:** Guardian of meaning & rules
### Task 1: Lock the Core Statement (45 min)

Write and refine **ONE sentence**:

- Why the brand exists
- Why are clothes earned
- What problem it solves

Example structure (don’t copy, just format):


Do not move on until this sentence feels solid.

---

### Task 2: Define the First Challenge Tier (1–1.5 hrs)

Create: - 5–7 **entry-level challenges**
- Only strength / endurance
- Clear numbers (no vague efforts)

For each challenge, write:
- Movement
- Standard (form, distance, time)
- Why it’s included
This becomes your **foundation gate**.

---

### Task 3: Standards You Will NOT Compromise (30–45 min)

Write a short list:
- What you will never sell
- What you will never allow
- What disqualifies someone

This protects the brand long-term.

---

## FOUNDER B — BRAND & SYMBOLISM (≈ 3 hours)

**Role:** Meaning translated into visuals & tone
### Task 1: Brand Tone Definition (45 min)

Answer in writing:
- 5 words the brand **is**
- 5 words the brand **is not**

Then write:
- How the brand talks
- How it never talks

This prevents cringe later.

---

### Task 2: Clothing Philosophy (1–1.5 hrs)

Define:
- What the first item is (tee / hoodie / cap)
- What it symbolizes
- Where branding appears (inside tag, stitching, etc.)
- What wearing it should _feel_ like

No designs yet — just intent.

---

### Task 3: Symbol / Mark Direction (30–45 min)

Brainstorm:
- Shapes (circle, line, rune, number, coordinate)
- Hidden meanings
- Tier-based markers

No logos yet — just **conceptual symbols**.

---

## FOUNDER C — COMMUNITY & SYSTEMS (≈ 3 hours)

**Role:** Human side & execution logic
### Task 1: Who This Is For / Not For (45 min)

Write two lists:
- “This brand is for people who…”
- “This brand is not for people who…”

Be brutally honest.

---

### Task 2: Verification Logic (1–1.5 hrs)

Decide:
- How challenges are submitted
- What proof is required
- What causes rejections
- How retries work

Keep it simple, fair, and strict.

---

### Task 3: Community Rules Draft (30–45 min)

Write:
- 5–7 rules for behavior
- Language that’s encouraged
- Language that’s banned

This sets culture early.

---

## FINAL 30–45 MINUTES (ALL TOGETHER)

**Merge & Align Session**
- Each founder presents:
    - What they decided
    - What they struggled with
- Resolve conflicts **using brand values**
    
- Document:
    - Final core statement
    - First challenge tier
    - Non-negotiables

Do **not** redesign or second-guess — just align.

---

## Output You Should Have By End of Session

If you don’t have these, you drifted:

✅ One clear brand purpose sentence  
✅ First challenge tier defined  
✅ Brand tone & philosophy  
✅ Verification rules (basic)  
✅ Community standards
